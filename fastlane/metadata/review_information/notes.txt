Sloglide is a private film and series log. No Sloglide account, purchase, subscription, or demo credentials are required. The queue, title history, and individual grades stay on this device. Title search needs a connection; the on-device shelf and manual title entry work offline.

On a fresh install, complete the three introduction pages with Next and Start, or tap Skip. On Tonight, tap Stash Rashomon, then Light, then Dismiss. Save the grade (1 through 5). Closing Grade also saves the displayed grade. The title appears in Recent and Finish log. Discover offers films, series, an on-device shelf, and Type a title. Queue offers Earlier and Later to change order.

Optional Game Center: Sloglide initializes Apple's Game Center connection at launch. The personal log remains usable if the reviewer declines sign-in, has no Game Center account, or is offline. Sign in using the device's Settings > Game Center, or tap Connect Game Center in Profile. Once connected, Profile > View achievements opens Apple's achievements dashboard. Retry sync repeats a failed progress upload. Finish a film and save its grade to earn First credits. Finish and grade a series to earn Series finale. Other milestones are 10, 50, and 100 films, and 10 saved grades. Existing graded history also counts when Game Center connects. Queued titles and a finish awaiting its grade earn nothing. Only achievement identifiers and percentage progress are sent to Game Center; titles and grades are not sent. No friends permission is requested.

Profile also opens Finished titles, Grades, Day marks, Undo finish, Finishing, and Settings. Undo last finish returns that title to the queue. Settings > Reset all data clears the local diary after confirmation. These actions do not revoke Game Center achievements. Contact is available in Profile and Settings. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt for Game Center.
